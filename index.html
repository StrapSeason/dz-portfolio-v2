About

I design coherent worlds for ambitious technology products.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer and art director based in Moscow. Since 2022, I’ve worked across AI, fintech, smart devices and real estate — leading brand decisions, designing digital products and building the visual output around them.

I’m most useful when a product is new, complex or still finding its voice. I work closely with founders and product teams, present creative decisions, conduct reviews and coordinate small design teams.
