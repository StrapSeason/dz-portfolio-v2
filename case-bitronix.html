Bitronix

Building a character-led visual universe for a fast-moving investment product — from daily content to motion and product communication.

RoleVisual Designer
PeriodOct 2024—Apr 2026
OutputVisuals · Robots · ≈50 films

The challenge

Make an investment product feel approachable, active and recognisable inside a crowded Telegram feed.

A visual world able to publish every day.

Bitronix needed more than campaign art. It needed a production language for product news, education, performance stories, advertising and community communication. I owned the visual layer and built a system that could stay recognisable while changing format and message constantly.

Character system

Risk became
personality.

Instead of explaining every strategy through abstract charts, the product could speak through a family of distinct characters.

Product communication

The same world had to explain what the product does — not only attract attention.

Characters carried users into a real interface.

I developed the visual communication around the product and contributed to parts of the application together with the team. Presentation and launch materials connected the robot profiles with deposits, trading behaviour, risk and the Telegram-native experience.

Motion engine

Not one film.
A constant output.

Approximately fifty motion pieces were produced across product communication, social media and advertising. The system had to support both character-led world-building and direct performance formats.
